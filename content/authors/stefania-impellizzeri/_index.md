Stefania Impellizzeri is Associate Professor in the Department of Chemistry and Biology at Toronto Metropolitan University, where she holds the **Jet Ice Research Chair in Sustainable Materials Chemistry** and serves as TMU's Dimensions Faculty of Science Lead for EDI in Research. Her research centres on the synthesis and application of nanomaterials — including quantum dots and nanoparticles — for sensing and photonic applications. Dr. Impellizzeri has trained 40+ HQP. Within QuantOmics she mentors trainees in nanomaterial-based quantum biosensor development (Stream 1), co-leads Course 1.2 — Quantum Nanotechnology for Life Sciences, and leads Course 1.5 — EDI in Precision Health.
