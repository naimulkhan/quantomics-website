**Project title:** AI-Driven Multimodal Data Integration for Precision Therapeutics

**Brief project description:** This project will explore the application of artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to integrate and analyze multimodal biomedical data, including genomic information and biosensor signals, to support the development of personalized therapeutics. The research will focus on developing predictive models that can identify meaningful patterns in complex biological datasets, improve the understanding of therapeutic responses, and contribute to AI-powered drug discovery and precision medicine.
