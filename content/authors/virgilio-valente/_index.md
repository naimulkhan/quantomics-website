Virgilio Valente is Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical, Computer, and Biomedical Engineering at Toronto Metropolitan University, where he leads the **Wireless Sensing Lab**. His research aims to develop miniaturized, energy-efficient CMOS/MEMS sensor systems for implantable and injectable wireless biosensor networks for real-time health monitoring and diagnostics. He has trained 15+ graduated HQP. Within QuantOmics, Dr. Valente co-leads Stream 1 (Quantum Probe Design and Fabrication), guiding trainees through the development of next-generation biosensing platforms, and co-leads and co-delivers Course 1.1 — Biosensor Engineering for Precision Health.
