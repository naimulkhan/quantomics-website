Amber Simpson is Director of the **Dianne and Irving Kipnes Health Research Institute** and an **Amii Fellow at the Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute** at the University of Alberta. Her research focuses on developing computational models to interpret complex biomedical data, with applications in cancer research and personalized medicine. With 50+ HQP trained, she serves as Institutional Lead at the University of Alberta. Within QuantOmics, Dr. Simpson co-leads Stream 3 (AI-Powered Therapeutic Design), mentoring trainees in applying AI to multimodal biomedical data, and teaches in Course 1.3 — AI in Genomics.
