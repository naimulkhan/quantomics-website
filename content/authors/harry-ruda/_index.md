Harry Ruda holds the **Stanley Meek Chair in Advanced Nanotechnology** in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Toronto. He brings expertise in photonics and the development of photonic sensor technology, with significant contributions to the field. Within QuantOmics he guides training in the design and fabrication of photonic components for quantum biosensors, enhancing their sensitivity and specificity (Stream 1), and co-teaches Course 1.1 — Biosensor Engineering for Precision Health.
