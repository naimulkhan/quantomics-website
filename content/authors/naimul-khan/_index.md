Naimul Khan is Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical, Computer, and Biomedical Engineering at Toronto Metropolitan University and Director of the **Multimedia Research Laboratory**. His research focuses on multimodal machine learning, integrating AI, imaging, and biosensing technologies for healthcare applications. With over $3M in research funding from NSERC, OCE, MITACS, and SSHRC, he has led projects on physiological signal analysis for arrhythmia, therapeutic virtual reality experience design with emotion recognition, and AI-driven diagnostics from neonatal ultrasound. Dr. Khan has trained over 50 HQP, with graduates advancing to roles in academia and industry. As **Program Director of QuantOmics**, he is responsible for the overall operation of the program, leads Stream 3 (AI-Powered Therapeutic Design), oversees curriculum development, and co-leads the Multimodal-Omics Data Integration Bootcamp.
