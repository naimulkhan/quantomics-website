Jacques Corbeil is Professor in the Department of Molecular Medicine at Université Laval and a member of **MILA, Quebec's AI Institute**. He previously held a **Tier 1 Canada Research Chair in Medical Genomics** (2003–2024). His research focuses on the integration of multi-omics data to understand complex diseases, utilizing AI and computational biology. Dr. Corbeil has an extensive HQP training record (50+) and a rich history of knowledge translation, with extensive business experience. His lab facilities, including organ-on-chip and computational resources, are invaluable for HQP training. Within QuantOmics, he serves as Institutional Lead at Laval, co-leads Stream 2 (Genomic Signal Integration), and teaches in Course 1.3 — AI in Genomics.
